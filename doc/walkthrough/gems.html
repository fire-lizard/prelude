Gems
| Max 5 Skill Points, 150 Drachs |

Background/Summary
Gems are transported from the mines of Jerrock directly to the Barrier. The Vangorii's lust for gems in their uncut form has always seemed strange to native Valley dwellers. Of late, the Vangorii's demand for uncut gems has exceeded the amount the shops can supply.

Player's Introduction
Speak with any number of the residents in the Barrier about gems.

Objectives
Find out who's smuggling gems.

Completing Objectives
1) There are two ways to begin this quest.

1.1) When the party speaks with Dell at the schoolhouse, he will tell them that he found a gem by the side of the road right outside the east of the Barrier. He will mention that he has tried to find more but that Zito is always around.


1.2) If the party has someone with at least 19 intelligence they will spot the bag by the tree outside the eastern gates of the Barrier. If they pick up the gems Rolo and Zito will be dead and the quest ends.
2) After the party speaks with Dell or sees the Gems they must approach Zito at the eastern gates while he's working (between the hours of 20 and 3).

3) Zito will attempt to sneak off, and the party must try to follow him. If the party has a stealth skill of at least 41 they will be able to follow him to the Lusty Strumpet +2SP

4) Speak with Ples, the hostess of the Lusty Strumpet, about the Vangesh customers.

5) When the party approaches Rolo he will give the party 150 drachs to stay quiet.

6) The party can still report Zito for smuggling +3SP
