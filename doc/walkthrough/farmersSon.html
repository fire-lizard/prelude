Farmer's Son
| Max 2 Skill Points and Minor Sword Skill Improvement |

Background/Summary
Garmer, the son of a local farmer, has dreams of becoming a Guardian. Saamel, his father, wants him to stay on the farm to help him and become a farmer.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Saamel.

Objectives
Convince Garmer to be a guardian Saamel that's it's for the best.

Completing Objectives
1) During the day Saamel can be found on his farm, which in the far northwest corner of Crossing East. Agree to help Saamel with his son.


2) Saamel's son Garmer can be found in the farmland north of the farm house. There are a few ways to handle Garmer.

2.1) If the party challenges Garmer to a sword test with a leader of a sword skill above 31, the leader will win. Garmer will be discouraged to follow his dreams and the party earns no skill points.
2.2) If the party challenges Garmer to a sword test with a leader of a sword skill below 31, the leader will lose. Encouraged by his victory, Garmer will run off to the Citadel.
3) When the leader speaks with Saamel, Saamel will be furious. But with a Charisma check of at least 16 and a speech skill of at least 31 the leader will convince him that it's for the best +2SP
