River Pilgrim
| Max 6 Skill Points, Sylkie's Tear |

Background/Summary
Rervas is a river pilgrim who has journeyed up the dark path to shoot down the great falls in a barrel. He isn't a very good craftsman, and the barrel he constructed will break if he goes over the falls in it.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Rervas.

Objectives
Return Rervas to his family safely.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Tarha in the logger cabins in Ironwood. She will explain her concern for what Rervas is doing.

2) Rervas can be found in the southern part of the Monastery. There are two ways to save him from killing himself in the dark falls.


2.1) If the party has a member with a tinker skill of at least 61 they will be able to successfully fix the raft +6SP
2.2) If the leader has pure river blood they must have a speech skill of at least 36 to convince him not to go. If the leader is not pure river they must have a speech of at least 56 to convince him not to go +6SP
3) Return to Tarha and she will give the party Sylkie's Tear
