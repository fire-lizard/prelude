Main Plot



25) Once inside there are 2 ways for the party to obtain the Caldus Ore:

25.1) If someone in the party has a stealth skill of at least 41, they can gain the trust of the miner's without being caught by the guardians of House Baern. If the party has this high stealth but speaks with any of the guards they will still be caught by the guards.


25.2) If the party is caught and brought to Zephyros, he will ask the party to infiltrate the miner's rebellion.


26) Before the miners as a whole will trust the party, the party first be trusted by Louisa, which can be done in two ways.


26.1) After Louisa has calmed down she will tell the party about Jano being sent to the Underground Work camp. Jano can be found in the mines. If there is a person with river blood in the party, he will ask them to take his ring back to his wife Tilda. Tilda can be found in one of the shacks outside the mine 4SP


26.2) Go to the Underground Work camp and the party will find the guardian Peleus beating up on Gyrraent. There are 3 ways to make Peleus leave Gyrraent alone.


26.2.1) If the leader has a strength of at least 22 they can successfully threaten Peleus 4SP


26.2.2) If the leader has charisma of at least 15 and speech of at least 36 they can convince Peleus to leave Gyrraent alone 4SP


26.2.3) The leader can also choose someone in the party to challenge Peleus to a fist fight. If the party member has at unarmed skill of at least 31 they will win the fight 4SP


26.2.4) The party can also pick a fight with Peleus and defeat him in combat.4SP


27) When the party returns to speak with Louisa she will tell the party that Llerwm is in need of help. There are 2 ways to help Llerwm.

27.1) If the leader has a speech of at least 71 and charisma of at least 21, they can convince him not to seek vengeance on the dwellers +6SP


27.2) The party can agree to help Llerwm by meeting him at 20 hours. At 20 hours Llerwm will take the party to fight a large number of tough cavern dwellers. Once defeated, he will give the party the Hammer of Purity +6SP


28) If the party returns with Llerwm alive they will be approached by Jeda, who will ask the party to meet her again at 4 hours. When the party returns at 4, she will take them to a meeting at Prali's Tavern they will learn that they are planning to rob the Armory of House Baern. Argant will give the party two choices, to take out the guards who are guarding the Armory or to rob the Armory itself. Once the party has made its choice they will be asked to meet Argant back at Prali's Tavern at 23 hours.

29) When the party meets Argant at 23 hours they will go off to their tasks:


29.1) Robbing the Armory: Disarm several traps and break into a number of locks. Both the traps and locks take random tinker skills to successfully manipulate. Around 50 tinker skill is safe. If the successfully breaks into one of the armory chests they will be able to take several weapons of the Flame for themselves. After they have unlocked the final chest, they will return to the miners. There is a time limit of about 2 hours, so do it as quickly as possible.


29.2) Killing the Guards: Kill 3 or 4 House Baern Guardians outside the Armory. There is a time limit of about 2 hours, so do it as quickly as possible.


30) When the party returns from successfully accomplishing their tasks Jeda will take them to meet Subei, the leader of the Rebellion. Subei will ask the party to kill Zephyros. At this point the party must choose whether to rat out the leader of the miner rebellion or kill Zephyros.

30.1) Rat out Subei: Now that the party knows who the leader of the rebellion is, they can return to Zephyros who will kill Subei. Zephyros will then give the party a box and tell them to bring it to Caryn in Land's End. If the party accepts, he will teach the party "Flame Sentinel" and allow them to leave Jerrock +10SP


30.1.1) Bring the box to Caryn in Land's End.


30.1.1.1) If the party brings Caryn the box and already completed Rebellion VI by killing Ciwdwr, Caryn will ask the party to speak with Gwyd. Gwyd will invite the party to become one with the darkness. If the party accepts, they will become creatures of the darkness. The Valley will be overrun by the darkness and have no hope. End of game.


30.1.1.2) If the party brings Caryn the box but did not complete Rebellion VI by killing Ciwdwr, Caryn, along with thugs and dark creatures, will ambush the party. If the party survives the ambush, they can return to Caryn's house where they will find the Caldus Ore.


30.2) If the party chooses to assassinate Zephyros, Subei will give the party "Subei's Axe." Zephyros, who can be found in his mansion, will be aided by Kheiron and Rhesos in the fight. Once the party has killed all three and walked out of Zephyros's mansion they will be approached by Subei. Subei will sneak them outside of the gates. Once outside he will improve party members Gift of Goddess skill and teach them the spell "Cycle of Life" and substantially +10SP


31) Now that the party has the Caldus Ore they must find the master smith Alves to forge the dagger. Travel to Kellen, which is now overrun with creatures of the darkness. Once the creatures of darkness have been defeated, Alves can be found in his shop. He will forge the Caldus Ore into the Novacula. After he has forged the Novacula, Alves will offer to join the party (he comes with a Cold Steel Hammer, a Cold Steel Breastplate and a Cold Steel Helm).



32) The party must now bring the Novacula, to be blessed by a priest of the flame. There are Temples in The Barrier, The Citadel, Ironwood and Lands End West. Any priest in the Valley will do. *IMPORTANT* The party can use the Novacula and Novacula Sanguineus in combat, but make sure that it is in a party member's inventory when brought to be blessed.


33) Once the party has the Novacula is blessed, it will become Novacula Sanguineus. The autotravel function will become disabled. The party will have to manually walk to the docks of Crossing East. Walking back from each of the 4 temples will have a number of various unique encounters filled with hordes of dark creatures. It will be very tough to rest, so make sure the party is well rested and equipped before leaving town!! *NOTE* If the party is travels from the Barrier and solved the Crazy Inventor quest, Verugi will appear and destroy the beasts.


35) When the party makes it to the docks of Crossing East they will be guarded by five Major Possessed. *NOTE* If the party solved the Priestess II without ratting out Vectrix, Vectrix will come with three zombies to aid the party in combat.


36) Once the Major Possessed have been defeated, take the raft to Goddess Isle Once again. Use the Novacula Sanguineus on the Alter in order to harness the blood magic and restore the Goddess's power.
