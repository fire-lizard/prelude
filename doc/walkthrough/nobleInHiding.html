Noble in Hiding
| Max 0 Skill Points, 1500 drachs |

Background/Summary
Lucius, a noble of House Baern has stowed himself away in his mansion, which is next to Zephyros's Mansion. He has feared for his life and the life of his family because he opposed Zephyros's decision to lock down Jerrock.

Player's Introduction
Enter Lucius's Mansion.

Objectives
Enter the room where Lucius is hiding and blackmail him.

Completing Objectives
1) The party can only speak to Lucius by getting into the room. There are three ways to enter.


1.1) If the leader has charisma of at least 20 and speech skill of at least 61 they will be able to convince him to open the door.
1.2) If there is a party member with strength of at least 21, they will be able to force the door.
1.3) If there is a party member with tinker of at least 31, they will be able to pick the lock.
2) Speak with Lucius and the leader can tell him that Zephyros sent them to kill him. He will offer the party 1000 drachs to leave him be. Raise it to 1500.
