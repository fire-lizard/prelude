Alian
| Max 9 Skill Points, NPC Bashnakh |

Background/Summary
On the night of his tenth birthday Alian had a dream where he was called by the flame. The next dawn, he packed some provisions, left a note for his parents, and departed for the Monastery of the Charred Ones. At the Monastery Alian went through the brutal initiation rituals to become One of the Singed, the first rank of monk. All of his possessions were burned and he was given the new name (from the flame tongue) of Bashnakh.

Player's Introduction
Outside the entrance to the Dark Path is a small farm. Speak with Paeia.

Objectives
Figure out who Alian is and bring him to his father as his dying wish.

Completing Objectives
1) Paeia will tell the party that it his father's dying wish to see his son Alian.


2) Speak with Rangard. If the leader has pure flame blood they will require a charisma of at least 11. If they are mixed or a river person they must have higher charisma respectively for Rangard to tell the members of the Monastery are named. If the leader has an intelligence of at least 15 they will be able to ask where the records are kept.

3) Zand of the Monastery will tell the party Alian is now Bashnakh +2SP


4) The leader that speaks with Bashnakh must have:

4.1) At least 33 speech if they are pure flame +3SP
4.2) At least 43 speech if they are mixed +3SP
4.3) At least 53 if they are pure river +3SP
5) When the party returns to Alian's Farm they will find his family dead. Bashnakh will meditate +4SP

6) Return in one week and Bashnakh will be through meditating. He will join the party.
