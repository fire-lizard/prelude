Garden Pest
| Max 2 Skill Points, Power of Flame Spell "Berserk" -OR- Gift of Goddess Spell "Shield of Self" |

Background/Summary
Thekla is tired of her garden being invaded and has set traps all around Ironwood to catch the culprit. One of the traps has the critter.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Thekla or see the bingo.

Objectives
Kill or free the bingo.

Completing Objectives
1) The bingo is located northwest of the Barracks. When the party encounters the bingo they will find it stuck in a trap and will have 2 choices.


1.1) With a tinker skill of at least 21 the party can successfully free the Albino Bingo. The Goddess will reward the party with the gift of Shield of Self 2SP
1.2) Kill the Albino Bingo and return to Thekla with its pelt. Thekla will reward the party with the Power of Flame spell Berserk 2SP
