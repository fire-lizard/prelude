The Huntsmen
| Max 7 Skill Points, Heartswood, 140 drachs |

Background/Summary
Brolund, the head of the huntsmen is short staffed because of bingo attacks.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Brolund who is in a cabin to the northeast of the Trusty Saw.

Objectives
Help the huntsmen.

Completing Objectives
1) Brolund first sends the party to act as an armed escort for his loggers. If the party accepts they will be attacked by bingos. The party must destroy the bingos without any loggers being killed. Once the bingos are dead the party will return to Brolund +2SP


2) Return to Brolund again and he will ask the party to rescue a group of loggers in northeast Ironwood. The party must kill all of the bingos surround the loggers cabin without the loggers dying. Return to Brolund +2SP


3) Brolund will now send the party to find and destroy the beast that killed on of his loggers. He will first mark the location of where he was killed on the Valley map. Go to the location of the body.

4) If the party has a member with nature skill of at least 26 they will be able to track the beast to its lair. If not, cross the river south of Ironwood and continue to follow the mountain wall south. The beast is in a cave a short distance southeast of Ironwood.

5) Go to the beast's lair, destroy the beast and collect its pelt.

6) Return to Brolund with the pelt and he will give the party Heartswood +3SP
