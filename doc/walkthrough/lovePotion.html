Love Potion
| Max 2 Skill Points |

Background/Summary
Sau and Aijo, two local farmers, bought a recipe for a love potion from a Vangesh woman who traveled through town. Their plan was to give the love potion to one of Aijo's daughters so she'd fall in love with Rarmer, Sau's ugly farm boy son. The fathers' bungled the brewing (making a stronger potion) which ended up in the entire family's soup. This gave all of the women in Aijo's house a super dose of the love potion, and both of Aijo's daughters and Aijo's wife are madly in love with Rarmer. Fearing for his son's life Sau has locked Rarmer in the house, and is trying to figure out what to do as he watches the three women try to claw their way through the locked door. Aijo is home drowning his sorrows in the bottom of a barrel.

Player's Introduction
As the party approaches Sau's farm he will see women trying to claw their way in.

Objectives
Figure out why the women are acting this way, prepare an antidote and bring it to them

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Sau at his farm. *WARNING* If the lock is picked before the Love Potion Antidote is administered the women will tear Rarmer apart, ending the quest.


2) Go back and forth between Sau and Aijo until Aijo informs the party that he gave the women a love potion from a Vangesh Merchant.

3) Visit the Barrier and speak with Mayaa. If the party has a member with intelligence of at least 10 they can ask her if she's been in Crossing East recently. There are 2 ways to get the recipe.


3.1) If the leader is Vangesh Mayaa will tell the party the recipe.
3.2) If the leader is not Vangesh they must bribe her 100 drachs for the recipe.
4) The recipe for the antidote is Cave Fungus and Doir Fruit. Cave Fungus can only be found in the cave to the northwest of the Barrier. The cave is filled with fungins and fungoids. The party can make the antidote themselves or have Mayaa make it for them (for more drachs).


5) When the party returns Sau's Farm and gives him the Love Potion Antidote, he will administer it to the women and they will be cured +2SP
