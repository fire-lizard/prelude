Fuzzy
| Max 2 Skill Points, Gift of Goddess Spell "Lethargy" |

Background/Summary
On a recent trip to Ironwood Irvari brought his son a fuzzy hatchling to keep as a pet. Fuzzy is older now, and none too fond of captivity.

Player's Introduction
Eian will come running to the party, begging for the party's assistance with his rampaging Fuzzy.

Objectives
Subdue or kill Fuzzy.

Completing Objectives
1) The party will be approached by Eian when they walk northeast of the Barrier's Temple of the Flame.


2) When the party follows him they will be transported to his house where Fuzzy has gone rampant.

3) If the party has someone with at least 26 nature skill, they will know to rub Fuzzy's belly to calm him. In order to catch Fuzzy and rub its belly a party member must have at least 15 dexterity. If the party does not have high enough nature and dexterity they must kill Fuzzy. Killing Fuzzy will earn the party +1SP

4) If the party calms Fuzzy, the Goddess will grant them Lethargy +2SP
