Crazy Inventor
| Max 7 Skill Points |

Background/Summary
Everyone in Kellen is convinced that Verugi is insane. Everyone in Kellen is right. Verugi is convinced that he is a great inventor. He claims to have created such brilliant innovations as "the table" and "the chair." He is currently working on a device called the Ultimate Power Source (UPS), which he believes will give him telepathic control of everyone in the Valley.

Player's Introduction
Go to Arvild's house.

Objectives
Help Verugi assemble the Ultimate Power Source.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Verugi. Kiss his feet and fetch him some milk from Vostari's Bakery.


1.1) If the leader has intelligence of at least 16 they can convince him that the milk is from Land's End by responding that he is never wrong +1SP
1.2) If the leader does not have an intelligence of 16 they will need to bring him another milk, which he'll accept +1SP
2) Fetch Verugi a Vangesh Bow. They can be bought from Trovis in the Barrier +1SP

3) Fetch 2 Bannanes for Verugi from Normin in the Barrier. He will eat the first one +2SP

4) Fetch a Black Gem from Telus in the Barrier +1SP

5) Fetch a Ball of Twine for Verugi from Vostari's Bakery +2SP
