Hostile Encounters


Almost all hostile encounters can be avoided by boosting the right skills. See the character creation/buildling section.

A Thaumaturgist and his Golem
Area(s): Roads
Description: Malorian and his golem attack. Malorian is frail, but his golem is very powerful. Thaumaturgists in the party can also learn the golem ritual (*SPOILERS*).


Bandits
Area(s): Anywhere
Description: Bandits will attack by surrounding and ambushing the party or, if the party stumbles into them, a random number of bandits will be placed in a random formation

Bandits attacking a Caravan
Area(s): Roads
Description: The party may help fight off a number of bandits who have ambushed a caravan.

    Fake Caravan Ambush
    Area(s): Roads
    Description: The party will stumble upon a caravan. The caravan master will ask the party for help with directions. If there is someone in the party with intelligence of at least 14 they will notice something unwholesome about the caravan master. If the party helps the caravan master with his map, they will be surrounded and ambushed. If they refuse to help, the caravan bandits will attack in scattered formation.
