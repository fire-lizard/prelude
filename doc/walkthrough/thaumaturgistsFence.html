Thaumaturgist's Fence
| Max 2 Skill Points, Thaumaturgical Ritual "Lightning Stone" |

Background/Summary
Cranos is an old Thaumaturgist hermit who lives in a cottage east of Kellen. He doesn't associate with the Kellen locals and likes to be left alone. He has a giant fence around his property. There is a rainberry orchard outside of his house which local kids have been raiding recently. He has been trying to scare them away by using lightning stones, but doesn't know how they are getting in. There is a small hole in the fence that the children have been using to sneak in.

Player's Introduction
See the hole in the fence or speak with children.

Objectives
Stop the child from throwing stones and fix the hole in the fence.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Kauro, who will be playing in the northeast corner of town during the day. She will tell the party of the mean man who threw lightening rocks. If the leader has an intelligence of at least 19 they will notice the rainberry stains on her hand. With a speech skill of at least 10 they will be able to convince the child to stop stealing the rainberries +1SP

2) Walk to eastern portion of the fence to notice the hole. There are 2 ways to fix the hole.


2.1) Buy hammer and nails from Vostari's Bakery. If party member has tinker skill of at least 10 they will be able to fix the fence.
2.2) Pay Arvild 30 drachs to fix the fence for them.
3) Once the fence is fixed the party can speak with Cranos and he will teach the party the thaumaturgical ritual Lightning Stone +1SP
