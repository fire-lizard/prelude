Missing Daughter
| Max 9 Skill Points |

Background/Summary
Aldir's daughter Hilmi is missing. In actuality Hilmi has been possessed by the darkness and has begun to lose control. Hilmi was abducted by other possessed so that she didn't make a scene and make the presence of the darkness known. She is currently chained in a basement in Crossing East.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Aldir.

Objectives
Bring Hilmi to Aldir and collect the reward.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Aldir who will tell the party about his missing daughter Hilmi.

2) Speak with Marjo of the Trusty Saw about the two strangers who were in town when Hilmi went missing.

3) Hilmi can be found in the cellar of a a farm in Crossing East. When the party opens the cellar door they will be attacked by Kirtto and Hrosta. Once the party has defeated Kirtto and Hrosta they may enter.


4) Speak with Hilmi and the party will be transported to Ironwood +5SP


5) Once the party returns to Ironwood with Hilmi the quest can end a few ways.

5.1) If the party has a member with at least 17 intelligence they will notice something wrong with Hilmi and attack her before she kills Aldir +4SP
5.2) If the party does not notice the strange look she will kill Aldir before they have a chance to attack her +2SP
