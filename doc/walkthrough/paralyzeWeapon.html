Paralyze Weapon
| Max 4 Skill Points, Thaumaturgical Ritual "Paralyze Weapon" |

Background/Summary
Girian, a respected professor and master of the thaumaturgical arts, can teach the party a number of powerful rituals.

Player's Introduction
After completing the Swift Weapon quest Girian will offer to teach the party Paralyze Weapon.

Objectives
Bring Girian River Dragon eggs.

Completing Objectives
1) Girian will mark the party's Valley map with the River Dragon Lair.

2) Travel to the River Dragon Lair. Defeat the River Dragons in combat. Collect their eggs from the back of the lair.


3) Return to Girian with the Dragon Eggs. Girian he will teach the Thaumaturgist Paralyze Weapon ritual and increase their thaumaturgy skill +4SP
