Lucky Charm
| Max 5 Skill Points, Power of Flame Spell "Haste" |

Background/Summary
Gavin borrowed a charm from his friend Aldrich to increase his luck with the ladies. Aldrich wants to hear nothing of his former friend until the charm is found. Gavin did not lose the charm, it was stolen by Daphne.

Player's Introduction
Gavin is in a shanty to the northeast of the Kaliyan Inn. When the party encounters him he will be searching for the charm.

Objectives
Find the charm and repair the friendship.

Completing Objectives
1) Talk to Gavin about the charm and the lady that he met at the Kaliyan Inn.


2) Go to the Kaliyan Inn and use a male party member speak with Daphne.


3) The party must impress Daphne in order to succeed in this quest. Daphne is pretty sensitive, so just remember to always be a gentleman... if not, the party will wake up with a serious problem! The first step in impressing Daphne is by bringing her a Strawberry Daiquiri. After that there are 4 ways to impress her:

3.1) If the leader has charisma of at least 17 she will immediately be impressed
3.2) If the leader has a pickpocket skill of at least 16 and dexterity of at least 13 they will successfully pull off a coin trick
3.3) If the leader has a literacy and lore skill of at least 16 they can tell Daphne a random fact about the Valley or recite a poem. If your leader recites a poem they must have speech skill above 25 for Daphne to be impressed. If the leader tells her a random fact she'll be impressed no matter the skill level of the leader.
4) Once she's impressed she'll make an enthusiastic offer to take the leader back to her room.

5) When brought back to the room Daphne will go change into something more comfortable, which will give the party the opportunity to look for the charm. The number of places that the leader can look through in the room is based on the leader's speed. The Lucky Charm is in the vase +2SP


6) The next morning the party can return to Aldrich, who can be found in a house northwest of the Barrier Temple. Present Aldrich with the charm and he will teach the party the Flame Spell Haste


7) Return to Gavin and tell him that Aldrich has forgiven him +3SP
