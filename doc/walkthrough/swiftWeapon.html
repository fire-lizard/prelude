Swift Weapon
| Max 3 Skill Points, Thaumaturgical Ritual "Swift Weapon" |

Background/Summary
Girian, a respected professor and master of the thaumaturgical arts, can teach the party a number of powerful rituals.

Player's Introduction
After completing the Harden Armor quest Girian will offer to teach the party Swift Weapon.

Objectives
Collect the components for the Swift Weapon ritual from Ludenius

Completing Objectives
1) Girian sends the party to assist Ludenius in collecting the components for the Swift Weapon ritual. Unfortunately, Ludenius cast a bad ritual and has lost his memory. The party must jog his memory so that Ludenius can give the party the components for the Swift Weapon ritual.

2) Ludenius stands gazing at the statue in the courtyard east of the fountain. There are 3 ways to jog Ludenius's memory. The party must do two of the three things in order for Ludenius to regain his memory. All of the clues are located in Ludenius's room, which is in the Academy dormitory.


2.1) Locked in the cabinet is Ludenius's Diary. Someone in the party must have tinker of above 30 to open the cabinet. Pick the lock and bring the diary to Ludenius.
2.2) There is a Note from Leris on Ludenius's desk. It's from his father in Lands End. Travel to Leris's house and Leris will come see his son.


2.3) There is a Recipe Book on the desk with a recipe for Ludenius's favorite cake. Purchase all of the required ingredients (Milk, Sugar, Flour, Eggs, Doir Fruit) from Sallio and bring them to the oven. Bake the cake and bring it to Ludenius.


3) Once they party has done any two of these three things Ludenius will regain his memory and tell the party that he hid the spell components in a box south of town +3SP

4) Open the box and inside the party will find 5 Spores. Return to Girian with the Spores and Girian will improve the Thaumaturgist's skill and teach them the Swift Weapon ritual
