The Fever
| Max 15 Skill Points, Thaumaturgical Ritual Knockout Dust |

Background/Summary
The most expensive restaurant in the Valley serves a unique cuisine prepared by a Vangorii chef. The local elite cannot get enough of the exotic delicacies. It is no coincidence that they are falling deathly ill. The disgruntled chef is poisoning them.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Nader, Alec or other Barrier southsiders.

Objectives
Discover what's causing the illness and have it cured.
Completing Objectives
1) By talking to almost any Barrier southsider (including Nader, Alec, or Saspina) the party learns that a few of the local elite have the same illness. There are two doctors in the Barrier, working separately to find a cure for the illness.

2) The party must get into the restaurant, which can be done 3 ways.

2.1) Get in Erynne's good favor.
2.1.1) Speak with Nader about how all elite are wearing Head Pieces.
2.1.2) Buy a Head Piece from Aines at Barrier Wears.
2.1.3) The leader must don the Head Piece and tell Alec that the party are dignitaries from out of town. Ask about the schoolhouse on the south side of town and Alec will inform the party that the host of the Mendos Restaurant Tristian has a crush on the teacher Erynne.


2.1.4) Go to Alersa's Stand in the market place and buy Milk
2.1.5) Speak with Erynne and give her the Milk and tell her that it was no trouble.+1SP


2.1.6) Now that the party is in Erynne's good favor, Tristian will let the party into the restaurant ahead of everyone else +2SP


2.2) Return Sarenti's heirlooms.
2.2.1) Speak with Sarenti about how his Inn was recently robbed of precious heirlooms.
2.2.2) Go to the shack directly to the southwest of the Barrier gates.
2.2.3) There are three ways to get the Mendos Brush and Mendos Mirror from Cladd
2.3.2.1) With pickpocket skill of around 20 it is possible to steal them from Cladd
2.3.2.2) If the party's reputation is low (below 980), Cladd will sell the party the heirlooms for 100 drachs
2.3.2.3) Initiate combat and defeat the thugs in combat.
2.2.4) Return to the Mendos Inn and give Sarenti the heirlooms. In exchange he will give the party the Sarenti Pass *WARNING* If the party accepts the Sarenti Pass they will not be able to complete the Kaliyan Inn quest.
2.3) Pickpocket Sarenti.
2.3.1) With a pickpocket skill of around 30 the party can steal the Sarenti Pass from Sarenti. The party can use this pass to get into the restaurant ahead of everyone else.
3) Once inside the restaurant the waitress will ask for the party's order. Ask about the House Special and the waitress will accidentally call the dish "Igboi." When the party asks what the slip was about, she will say that Akwasi calls the special Igboi. Order the Akwasi Special and exit the restaurant +1SP


4) Speak with Mayaa, the Vangesh spice vendor in the marketplace. Unless speaking with a Vangesh member the, party will have to bribe Mayaa 40 drachs (adjusted for leaders with higher charisma) for any information on the dish. She will then inform the party that it is only made in a small place in the far east that she is not very familiar with, but that a Vangorii woman from that region works at the Lusty Strumpet +2SP


5) When the party speaks with Roh she will explain that one of the spices that gives Igboi its unique flavor can be very poisonous. She mentions a chef who had to flee because he was poisoning people +3SP


6) If the party has no one with intelligence above 9 the quest ends here. If there is someone in the party with intelligence above 9, they will realize that the Vangorii chef of the Mendos Restaurant is the same as the one Roh spoke of and that he is poisoning people. From here there are two ways to solve the quest, through the local doctor Argus or through the visiting doctor Crallius.

6.1) Speak with Argus and he will inform the party that he has been working with "The Fever" for quite some time. He hasn't been able to make any progress in figuring out what the cause of the fever is, let alone the find cure. When the party informs him that Akwasi has been poisoning officials he will ask for a sample of the dish. Once the party has given him the dish, Argus will reward the party with the thaumaturgical ritual Knockout Dust +6SP


6.2) Crallius is located in Darien and Saspina's house in the southside of the Barrier. Inform Crallius of the poisonings and give him the dish. He will then cure Darien and head back to the Citadel leaving it in the hands of the party to inform the local guardians. Now the party can talk to Vortia, who will accompany her guardians to arrest Akwasi and bring him back to the southside barrack's holding cell +3SP
