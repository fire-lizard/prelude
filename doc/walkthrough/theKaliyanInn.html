The Kaliyan Inn
| Max 2 Skill Points |

Background/Summary
The Kaliyan Inn was purchased by Ermis over a decade ago. He changed the name, but nobody has any idea why.

Player's Introduction
When the party asks Dinea, the host of the Kaliyan Inn, she will mention that she does not know where the name of the inn came from.

Objectives
Discover how the Kaliyan Inn got its name.

Completing Objectives
1) Dinea will mention that the owner's of the inn has a daughter named Telen, and that she is a drunk who's usually passed out by the bar.

2) Telen can be found laying on the floor on the south side of the main room. Speak with her and the party will learn that she's suffering from a hangover.


3) Talk to Sarenti, the proprietor of the Mendos Inn about the Kaliyan Inn. He will inform the party that people from Kaliyan come to him for his hangover remedy. Speak to him again and he'll mention that his inn was recently robbed of some precious heirlooms. The thugs who robbed him can be found in a small shack directly to the southwest of the Barrier gates.


4) There are three ways to get the Mendos Brush and Mendos Mirror from Cladd.

4.1) With pickpocket skill of around 20 it is possible to steal them from Cladd
4.2) If the party's reputation is low (below 980), Cladd will sell the party the heirlooms for 100 drachs
4.3) Initiate combat and defeat the thugs in combat


5) Once the party has the heirlooms they can return to the Mendos Inn. As a reward for bringing back the heirlooms he will give the party the Hangover Remedy.

6) Return to Telen with the Hangover Remedy. After she has taken the remedy she will tell the party where the name of the Inn came from +2SP
