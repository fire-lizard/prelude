The Archives
| Max 3 Skill Points, Ignatian Platemail, Longsword, 5 gems, 500 drachs, Seppus's Helm -OR- Ignatian Battle Gloves -OR- Zorella's Ring |

Background/Summary
The Monastery's archives consist of a system of underground tunnels, which have become overwhelmed with aggressive and unfriendly cavern dwellers. No one has dared set foot in them in decades.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Bazir about retrieving the Sanguineus Codex

Objectives
Clear out the Cavern Dwellers

Completing Objectives
1) Once the party has permission from Bazir to enter the Archives (see, Main Plot), Virmorn will them to clear the Archives of all of the dwellers.

2) There are lots of dwellers of all kinds in the Archives, and a large number of valuables as well. Many gems, drachs, and items (including the most powerful armor in the game, Ignatian Plate Mail) can be found. Opening some of the doors requires keys in which the party must clear out the dwellers before having access to.


3) Return to Virmorn and he will give the party the key to the storage chamber in the archives +3SP

4) Go back into the archives and the storage chamber is due north. The party can take either Seppus's Helm -OR- Ignatian Battle Gloves -OR- Zorella's Ring. If the party takes more than one of the items, all of the monks will attack.
