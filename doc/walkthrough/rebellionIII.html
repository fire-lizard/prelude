Rebellion III
| Max 0 Skill Points |

Completing Objectives
1) Genwen will ask the party to switch guardian's orders.

2) Go to the Treefort Inn and buy 5 beers from Borunn.

3) Use a party member with pickpocket skill to give Lykodemes the beers. Once the party has given Lykodemes 5 beers he will pass out.


4) Once Lykodemes is passed out the leader only needs a pickpocket skill of 3 to successfully switch the orders.

3) Return to Genwen with the Guardian Orders.
