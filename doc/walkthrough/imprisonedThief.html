Imprisoned Thief
| Max 2 Skill Points, NPC Denomas -OR- Minor Improved Pickpocket and Tinker Skill |

Background/Summary
Denomas, a master thief, was caught trying to steal Aldir's reward money.

Player's Introduction
Walk into the Ironwood Barracks.

Objectives
Sneak Denomas out of prison.

Completing Objectives
1) Walk into corridor where prisoners are held in the Ironwood Barracks and Denomas will ask the party to come speak with him.


2) Speak to Denomas by talking through the south most prison door.

3) By day speak with Janne about Trillis's drinking habits. Then buy 4 Jugs of Ale from her.

4) Between the hours of 22 and 9 bring the Jugs of Ale to the guardian Trillis. After four drinks he'll pass out.


5) If anyone in the party has pickpocket skill they can now easily steal Trillis's Jail Key. Use the key to free Denomas.

6) When freed Denomas will mark the party's Valley map with the Marker Stone (which is a short distance west of Ironwood) and then depart +2SP

7) Meet Denomas at the Marker Stone and he will offer to join the party or train them in pick pocketing and tinker. *WARNING* Denomas will not have any interest in coming anywhere near to Ironwood if he joins the party.
