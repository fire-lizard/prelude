Geris
| Max 3 Skill Points, Thaumaturgical Ritual "Golem" |

Background/Summary
Beneath Crossing East is a small tunnel complex the ancients used as a holding area for dangerous or unwanted people and creatures. In order to safely transport the creatures the ancients designed a transporter system that involved pulling a level which would activate the transport pad on the bridge. The guardian and janitor of the complex is a Golem, which now stands in its alcove waiting for someone to come and activate it. A flame blood scholar named Geris has recently come to Crossing East to study the bridge and ancient ruins. He has figured out that the lever activates something on the bridge, but he can't be at both places at once and he hasn't been able to talk any of the locals to help him with his research.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Geris, who can be found north of Mirko's General.

Objectives
Find out for Geris what's in the Ancient Cavern.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Geris, if the leader has a literacy and lore skill of at least 40 he will ask them to check the ancient cavern.


2) Find the Grey Gem, the Rust Gem, and the Magenta Gem scattered throughout the ruins. They are not hard to find, but are guarded by many mutants and red mutants.


3) Return to the Ancient Golem and place the Grey Gem, the Rust Gem, and the Magenta Gem into its forehead. The Ancient Golem will pull the lever and the party will return to the Crossing East. *WARNING* do not put any other gem into its forehead or he will attack and the quest ends.


4) Return to the Valley and speak with Geris. He will teach the party's thaumaturgist(s) the powerful thaumaturgical summoning ritual of Golem +3SP
