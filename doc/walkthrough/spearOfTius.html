Spear of Tius
| Max 2 Skill Points, Power of Flame Spell "Spear of Tius" -or- Tius's Spear |

Background/Summary
Racial tensions in the Barrier are mounting. While the legend of General Tius is revered by the Children of the Flame, River folk and the Vangorii see the statue as a symbol of their oppression.

Player's Introduction
When the party speaks to the Barrier's resident priest he will inform the party that the spear has been stolen from the statue of Tius at the center of town.

Objectives
The Spear of Tius is guarded by a small pack of thugs in the Barrier Sewers. The party can keep the Spear of Tius, but if they do not return it to Menanius they will not receive the spell or the +2SP. If the party does return the spear they will be granted the Power of Flame spell "Spear of Tius." *WARNING* there are some blocking issues with the sewers.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Menanius about the Spear of Tius

2) Retrieve the spear from the Barrier Sewers. It is to the southeast of the southern entrance being guarded by bandits. Beware of fungins, fungoids, hoppers and eyedrips.


3) Return to Menanius for the Power of Flame Spell "Spear of Tius" and +2SP or keep Tius's Spear
