Shepherd's Flock
| Max 5 Skill Points, Dirming's Crook |

Background/Summary
Dirming, a bakki herder, is distraught because a pack of lupins is feeding on his flock. The wolves used to eat the topi close to their den, but a hunter named Hjor recently began hunting the topi there and the lupins are starving.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Dirming.

Objectives
Stop the lupins from killing Dirming's flock.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Dirming about his flock.


2) Travel to the lupin cave which is in north location. From here there are 2 ways to solve the problem.


2.1) Stop Hjor.
2.1.1) Walk to the mouth of the cave. If the party has someone with nature skill of at least 10 they will be able to realize that the lupin are eating the bakki because of the topi shortage.
2.1.2) Speak with Hjor the hunter who is Olvar's in during the evening.


2.1.2.1) If the party has a member with an intelligence of at least 17 they will figure out that Hjor is hunting the topi that the lupins used to feed on, and that this is what's causing the lupins to attack Dirming's flock +1SP
2.1.2.2) If the leader has speech skill of at least 10 they will be able to successfully convince Hjor to stop hunting near the lupin den +1SP
2.2) Kill the lupins in the cave.
3) Return to Dirming to let him know that his flock should be safe he will give the party Dirming's Crook +3SP
