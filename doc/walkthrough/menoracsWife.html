Menorac's Wife
| Max 11 Skill Points, Priest Blade |

Background/Summary
Menorac's wife Rolucia began voicing her concerns about her husband Menorac's bodyguard Blaen at about the same time when Menorac was looking for a necklace to give her for their tenth anniversary. Seeing an opportunity to silence his opposition, Blaen found a necklace and had it enchanted by a Thaumaturgist to put its wearer into a coma. Blaen orchestrated it so that it was this necklace that Menorac gave to his wife. Three days after their anniversary Rolucia fell into a coma.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Menorac.

Objectives
Figure out what's wrong with Rolucia and find a cure.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Menorac. If everyone in the party has a charisma above 6, he will allow them to go into Rolucia's room to view her.

2) When the party enters the room they will find Rolucia in a coma, wearing a necklace. *WARNING* if the party removes the necklace without the Ferrin Solution, she will die and the guardians will hang the party. There are 2 ways to figure out that the necklace and enchanted and how to remove it.


2.1) If the party has a member with thaumaturgy skill above 40 they will be able to figure out that the necklace is enchanted and needs Ferrin and Springwater to remove the enchantment +4SP
2.2) If no one with thaumaturgy skill of at least 40 is in the party.
2.2.1) Visit Ternia the jeweler who will mark the party's map with Ancus's Cabin.
2.2.2) Go to Ancus's Cabin to find his remains. If the party has literacy and lore skill they can read his journal to find out that the solution to remove the enchantment is Springwater and Ferrin +1SP


3) Ternia will sell the party a Jar of Ferrin for 50 drachs and mark the location of the spring on the party's Valley map.

4) Go to the spring to retrieve the Springwater.

5) Mix the Jar of Ferrin and Springwater to make the Ferrin Solution.

6) Apply the Ferrin Solution to Rolucia's Necklace. Rolucia will wake and tell Menorac what happened. After they speak, Menorac will ask the party to accompany him to speak with Blaen. (If the leader has an intelligence of at least 18 they can ask Menorac to have guards accompany them) +4SP

7) Defeat Blaen and Irjo in combat and Menorac will give the party Priest Blade +3SP
