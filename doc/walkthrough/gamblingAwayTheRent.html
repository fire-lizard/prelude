Gambling Away the Rent
| Max 11 Skill Points |

Background/Summary
Odmi is facing eviction because her husband, a mill worker named Eirir, gambles all of his money away at the casino.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Odmi.

Objectives
Take care of Eirir and Odmi's rent and stop Eirir from gambling.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Odmi about Eirir gambling away the rent. There are a few ways to absolve the family of their debt.


1.1) Pay Somnius the rent for +1SP
1.2) Trick Somnius.
1.1.1) Go to Somnius's office in the Casino. Somnius does not remember who owes him what and will ask the party to bring him his rent ledger.
1.1.2) On Somnius's desk is a rent ledger and a pencil. If the leader has both intelligence and dexterity of at least 13, and literacy and lore of at least 25 they will be able to successfully forge Somnius's signature signifying that Odmi paid the rent +3SP


1.1.3) Bring the ledger to the Somnius and he'll think that Odmi already paid the rent.
1.3) Speak with Eirir and if the leader has charisma of at least 16 and speech of at least 48 they will be able to convince him to stop gambling.
1.4) Speak with Eirir and if the leader has strength of at least 17 they will be able to scare him into not gambling anymore.
1.5) Alternatively if the leader has a charisma of at least 13 and a speech of at least 31 they will be able to convince Menorac to close the Casino +2SP
2) Return to Odmi and tell her of what the party has done +6SP
