Masseque
| Max 16 Skill Points, Derrack's Axe, Khalil's Ring, Druid Staff, NPC Masseque -OR- Masseque's Bow |

Background/Summary
The Barrier is a cultural locus, bringing people from all over the Valley to see things they could only dream of in the smaller farm towns. But with cultural diversity and progressive thought comes political conflict. Nina, a young and capricious official, took an aggressive stance on raising landowner taxes, which did not gel well with the council. Despite the fact that it benefited the Barrier's economy as a whole, all of the landowners were outraged, but none so irate as Marta.

Marta succumbed to the Darkness long ago. With news of the tax she hired three thugs, Nills, Gram and Corin to ransack Nina's Mansion. The thugs tore the house apart and stole her belongings without leaving any incriminating evidence behind. After robbing Nina's the thugs decided that they were underpaid, so they showed up at Marta's to forcibly negotiate more payment. Marta disarmed Nills of his dagger and used it to slice him to pieces. Gram and Corin, who had been waiting in the other room, heard the screaming and ran to the door to see the frail old woman digging through the innards up their compatriot's lifeless body. They ran for their lives.

Gram was able to escape and is now hiding in caverns north of the Barrier. Corin was not so lucky. Marta followed and quickly dispatched of him. After dumping Corin's body into the sewers, Marta returned home and picked up Nills's corpse. On the way to the sewer entrance, she came upon a Vangorii man passed out in an alley after a night of hard drinking. She laid Nills's body next to him and slipped Nills' knife into his hand. When someone came upon the alley next morning they summoned the Guardians who arrested Masseque for murder. He is to be hanged shortly.

Player's Introduction
Walk into the northside barracks and Masseque will call to the party for help

Objectives
Find if Masseque is guilty. If he his innocent free him.

Completing Objectives
1) When the party enters the northside barracks Masseque will call for help. If the party tries to speak with him (by clicking on his cell door) the guards will not allow it.


2) Speak with Senzo about Masseque to find out about who is in the cell and why he's there.

3) Speak with Guidian about the ongoing feud between the guardians in the north and southside guardians. Guidian will call the guardians of the southside barracks incompetent wretches.

4) Go to the southside barracks and tell Estabon what Guidian said. Estabon will have some unpleasant things to say about Senzo's girlfriend.

5) Return to the northside barracks and tell Senzo what Estabon said. The northside and southside guards will then clear out of the barracks to rumble. This still leaves Derrack guarding the door +1SP


6) Go to the Lusty Strumpet and speak with the hostess Ples. If the leader has charisma of at least 16 Ples will inform them that Derrack frequents the establishment against protocol.


7) There are 2 ways to get around Derrack and speak with Masseque

7.1) Blackmail Derrack into letting the party talk to Masseque +1SP
7.2) Blackmail Derrack into giving the party Derrack's Axe
7.2.1) Go to the market place during the day and speak with Damrush about Derrack going against protocol.
7.2.2) Return to Derrack's office and Damrush will come to take Derrack into custody, freeing the party to speak with Masseque *NOTE* If the party rats Derrack out he will attack them in the alley south of the Kaliyan Inn.


8) Go to the northside barracks and speak with Masseque. He tells the party that he has been falsely charged with murder. He does not know who would do this or why, but he directs the party to speak with the people at the Kaliyan Inn, the scene of the crime *IMPORTANT* the party must offer to help him unconditionally or have a member with at least 15 charisma for him to join. The guards will now return and shoo the party from the cell +1SP


9) Go to Alersa's stand in the market place and buy some Milk, and then head to the Kaliyan Inn to speak with Dinea. In exchange for the Milk she will tell the party that the night before the murder Masseque was jumped by Nills and two of Nills's friends +1SP She doesn't remember the name of Nills's friends, so she directs the party to speak with Danton.
