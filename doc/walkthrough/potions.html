Potions, Salves and Draughts



Black Sarantim: Boosts the user's normal and dark attack resistance by 10% for 30 minutes. Also boosts the user's endurance by 2 for 30 minutes.

Elixer of Life: Restores 50% of the user's health.

Druid's Tea: Boosts willpower by 3 for 2 hours.

Eryni Peppers: Boosts the user's dexterity by 3 for 2 hours.

Flame Fungus: Boosts the user's Power of Flame skill by 5 for 2 hours.

Goddess Salve: Boosts Gifts of Goddess by 5 for 2 hours.

Healer's Salve: Restores 20% of the user's health.

Hearthwort: Boosts speed, dexterity, endurance and strength by 4 for 2 hours, but decreases charisma by 10 for 1 day.

Hegel Sap: Boosts strength by 6 for 2 hours, but decreases normal resistance by 50% by 2 hours.

Herbal Draught: Boosts dexterity, strength and speed by 1 for 2 hours.

Herbal Poultice: Boosts the user's dexterity and strength by 3 for 2 hours.

Speed Brew: Boosts the user's speed by 3 for 2 hours.

Star Petals: These petals only blossom in the presence of the Goddess. By ingesting the petals the user will boost both their intelligence and charisma by 6 for 1 day.

Strength Brew: Boosts the user's strength by 3 for 2 hours.

Witch Brew: Boosts the user's normal and dark attack resistance by 15% for 2 hours.

Witch Grass: Boosts the user's normal and dark attack resistance by 5% for 2 hours.

Universal Brew: Boosts strength, speed, willpower, dexterity and endurance by 2 for 2 hours.

Vial of Darkstrength: Makes the user 20% less vulnerable to dark attacks for 1 hour.
