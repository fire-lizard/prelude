Main Plot



13) Give Ciarius's Documents to Bazir, who is in a small house next to the dorms. He will tell the party that they must retrieve a full copy of the text in order for him to translate the document. The only full copy can be found in the Monastery Archives.


14) With Bazir's permission, Virmorn will allow the party to enter the Archives. The Sanguineus Codex is located in one of the chambers. There are 2 ways to retrieve it:


14.1) If a party member has a tinker skill of around 50, they will be able to pick the lock to the door.
14.2) The party can go around to the other side, which is guarded by several dwellers.
15) Bring the Sanguineus Codex to Bazir for him to translate +2SP. The party will immediately be summoned by Sharag. Sharag destroys Khal and teaches the party Power of Flame Spell "Fireball". He then informs them of the Darkness and that the only hope lies with the Goddess. He tells the party to take Jerahd Root in order to induce a vision of the Goddess.


16) Clear the few low level dwellers in the Monastery Garden and use the Jerahd Root. The party will have a vision with the Goddess telling them to come to her.

17) Travel to Crossing East to make a pilgrimage to the Goddess. To build a raft to get to Goddess Isle the party must first procure Ironwood. There are 2 ways to do so:

17.1) Pay 2000 drachs for Ironwood at Mirko's General.+2SP


17.2) Speak with Gralwn the carpenter who is located in the shop north of Mirko's General. He will tell the party that there is an Ironwood tree in Crossing East which is protected by the druid Falesin. Speak with Falesin and he will tell the party that he will part with his ironwood tree in exchange for a sapling. The sapling can be bought from Auno at the Mill Company Store in Ironwood for 400 Drachs. Bring the Ironwood Sapling back to Falesin and he will give the party his Ironwood +2SP


18) Before the party can build a raft, the Ironwood must to be softened. Falesin's Glade is filled with Alien Beasts. He will mark on the location of his glade on the party's Valley map. Once the party has destroyed the Alien Beasts they can return to him and he will soften the Ironwood for them.


19) Now that the party has the Softened Ironwood, there are 2 ways to build the raft.

19.1) Bring the Softened Ironwood to the docks of Crossing East. If someone in the party has a tinker skill of at least 21, they will successfully build the raft +2SP


19.2) Take the Softened Ironwood to Gralwn who will build it for 100 drachs.


20) When the raft has been built successfully it will appear on the docks of Crossing East. Use it to visit the Goddess.


21) Speak to the Goddess and she will tell the party that she does not have the power to rid the Valley of the Darkness. She will then give the party Star Petals and teleport them back to Sharag +1SP


22) Speak with Sharag of the Goddess's weakness. He will teach the party about blood magic and send the party to speak with Bazir about how to construct the blade that can unlock its power +2SP

23) Bazir will inform the party that the Novacula Sanguineus must be forged from Caldus Ore, which can only be found in Jerrock. He will give the party five Vials of Darkstrength

24) There are five ways to get into Jerrock.

24.1) If the party has been sent by Bargilof, they can tell Kheiron that they were sent to speak with Zephyros. For more information on Land's End Rebellion quests +5SP


24.2) Kheiron will tell the party that Gaston the Courier is the only outsider who's allowed beyond the gates of Jerrock.


24.2.1) Speak with the head courier Rialli at the Academy. He will tell the party that he's worried about Gaston because he is too old to be a courier. Rialli marks the party's Valley map with the location of Gaston's Cabin and asks the party to talk some sense into him.


24.2.2) Travel to Gaston's Cabin which is surrounded by River Dragons. Destroy the River Dragons.


24.2.3) Enter the cabin and speak with Gaston. He will give the party the Council Notice to deliver the rest of the way to Jerrock 2SP


24.2.4) Travel to Jerrock and give the council notice to Kheiron. He will bring the party to Zephyros of House Baern. +5SP


24.3) If a party member has a strength of at least 21 they will be able to bash their way in through a loose panel on the north side of the gates.+5SP


24.4) If the party has Rope and someone in the party with at least 22 dexterity they will be able to climb over the fence using the tree and let the rest of the party in.+5SP


24.5) Between the hours of 21 and 1 if there is a party member with stealth skill at least 36 the party can sneak in through the front gates.+5SP
