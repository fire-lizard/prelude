Eino
| Max 2 Skill Points, Gift of Goddess Spell "Mother's Kiss" |

Background/Summary
A small child named Eino was passing the time throwing rocks into the river and accidentally hit and wounded a Sylkie, one of the goddess's favorite creatures. He is scared that he will be punished, so he is hiding out in the woods. His parents Gralvar and Alena are worried and are looking for him.

Player's Introduction
Speak with Gralvar or Alena.

Objectives
Find Eino, heal the Sylkie, and bring Eino home.

Completing Objectives
1) Speak with Gralvar or Alena about their missing son.

2) Travel to just northeast of Salmor's Farm to find Eino.


3) There are a 3 ways to finish the quest from here.

3.1) Heal the Sylkie.
3.1.1) If the leader has speech skill of 10 the party will learn that Eino is hiding because he accidentally wounded a Sylkie and is scared that his parents will be mad with him.
3.1.2) Travel to the wounded Sylkie, who is still by the river


3.1.3) If someone in the party has Gift of Goddess skill or medical skill the party will be able to heal the Wounded Sylkie. The Goddess will grant the party "Mother's Kiss".
3.1.4) Return to Eino and tell him that the Sylkie is healed. He will return to his parents +2SP


3.2) Grab Eino. If the leader has a dexterity of at least 18 they will be able to grab Eino and bring him home +2SP
3.3) Tell on Eino. If the leader does not have high enough dexterity they can tell Gralvar where Eino is +1SP
